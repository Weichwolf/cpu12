Native multiplication and division

MUL (12), EMUL (13), EMULS (18 13), IDIV (18 10), IDIVS (18 15), EDIV (11),
EDIVS (18 14) and FDIV (18 11) compile directly into host integer instructions.
No ISA helper, memory/peripheral callback or native temporary frame is used.
The existing MUL;ADCA #0 rounding sequence uses the actual generated carry.

MUL changes only C, from result bit 7. EMUL/EMULS produce Y:D, update N/Z
from the entire 32-bit product and C from bit 15, and preserve V and upper CCR.
Division updates the quotient and remainder as specified by the pinned
S12CPUV2 entries. Unsigned/fractional 16-bit results retain N; signed/extended
results derive N/Z from the 16-bit quotient. Normal results clear C/V.
All operations preserve other registers, upper flags and instruction-boundary
fault/state contracts; guest division by zero retires with its documented C
status rather than becoming an unrelated host execution fault.

Zero-divisor guards precede native DIV/IDIV. IDIVS uses a 32-bit host divide,
so the guest 8000/ffff overflow can publish X=8000,D=0,V=1,C=0 without trapping.
EDIVS uses a 64-bit host divide, including signed 32 minimum divided by -1;
its guest 16-bit overflow preserves D/Y and sets V while clearing C. EDIV
rejects an oversized quotient before dividing. A biased unsigned range check
classifies EDIVS signed 16 quotients; a Z3 bit-vector proof covers every possible
quotient, with two SAT negative controls. These are arithmetic bounds only,
not proof of the entire CPU or hardware equivalence.

Architecturally indeterminate fields have a deterministic, explicit policy.
IDIV division by zero saturates X=ffff, retains the indeterminate D and sets
C while clearing Z/V. FDIV overflow/zero saturates X=ffff, retains the
indeterminate D, clears Z and sets V; C reports zero divisor. EDIV/EDIVS
zero and IDIVS zero preserve their specified input registers and retain the
undefined N/Z/V while setting C. EDIV/EDIVS overflow preserves D/Y and the
undefined N/Z while setting V and clearing C. IDIVS 8000/ffff produces the
low-word quotient and zero remainder; its undefined N/Z follow that low word.
Keeping an indeterminate remainder or choosing undefined flags is core policy,
not a silicon-measured fact. A firmware dependence on such fields requires
additional compatibility evidence.

Tests separately check all defined fields and the chosen policy for undefined
ones. All MUL byte pairs/CCRs, full word domains against directed other
operands, separate extended-dividend/divisor axes, all CCRs at boundary cases,
PC wrapping, zero divisors and signed overflow are covered with independent
integer expectations. No manufacturer application program is embedded.
The 48287746 current cases and fourteen earlier coherent consumers passed.
Of those new cases, 9438977 also check indeterminate policy; all cases check
their specified fields. multiply-divide-reference.json pins current sources,
binaries, actual exits, logs and the narrower arithmetic proof. Remaining ISA,
reset/IRQ/waits, fetch/native regions, checkpoints,
cycle budgets and Blofeld integration remain open. Component tests do not
establish firmware boot, complete instrument operation or real-time throughput.
